## Why Kinexus CPAs shares proactive tax and advisory insights

Most tax content online gets published once a year, right before a deadline, and then disappears. That's not how we operate, and it's not how the businesses on this page grow. Kinexus CPAs works with contractors, restaurant operators, real estate investors, medical practices, and service-based businesses across St. George, Utah, on a year-round basis, which means our advisory work doesn't stop after the return is filed.

Our team, including Shawn T.
