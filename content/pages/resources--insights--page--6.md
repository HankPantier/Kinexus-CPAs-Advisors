## Why our team writes about proactive tax and business strategy

Most accounting content online is written to fill a keyword quota. Ours isn't. Shawn Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and the rest of the Kinexus team write about the same issues we're actively solving for clients: WIP schedule accuracy for a Washington County contractor, entity structuring for a new restaurant group, quarterly estimates for a physician moving from W-2 to 1099 income.

We don't believe tax work should happen once a year in March. Planning happens in July, October, and January, whenever a decision actually changes the outcome. That's the philosophy behind every post here: fewer surprises, more decisions made with real numbers in hand. If a topic reads like it came from a CFO conversation rather than a tax prep checklist, that's intentional.
