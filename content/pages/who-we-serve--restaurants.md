## Accounting built for the realities of running a restaurant

Thin margins, cash drawers that need reconciling every night, and a staff roster that turns over every few months: restaurant accounting has almost nothing in common with bookkeeping for a retail shop or a law office. Prime cost has to be tracked weekly, not quarterly, or a bad week in food cost eats the whole month's profit. Kinexus CPAs & Advisors works with restaurant and food & beverage operators across St. George and Washington County, from single-location cafes to multi-unit groups managing tourist traffic near Zion National Park and Sand Hollow. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and [the team](/who-we-are/team) built this practice around kitchens and dining rooms, not spreadsheets in isolation. That means we ask about your POS system, your labor percentage, and your walk-in cooler lease before we ever open QuickBooks.

## Daily bookkeeping and back-office support for restaurants

Restaurant books live or die on daily reconciliation. Kinexus connects directly to your POS system, whether that's Toast, Square, or Aloha, and reconciles daily sales, credit card batches, and cash drops against what actually hit the bank. Vendor bills get coded and paid on schedule so your food distributor and linen service don't put you on credit hold mid-week. Inventory counts feed into cost of goods sold tracking so you know your food cost percentage by week, not by guessing at month-end. Our [restaurant bookkeeping](/what-we-do/bookkeeping) sets up QuickBooks to roll individual store performance into one consolidated view, so an owner running three St. George locations can compare labor and food cost side by side. For groups managing more than one location, see our approach to [multi-location and franchise tax strategy](/industries/restaurants/multi-location-franchise-tax-strategy).

## Payroll and tip reporting for restaurant teams

Tipped wages are one of the most audited parts of restaurant payroll, and one of the easiest places to get penalized for a small mistake. Kinexus [payroll services](/what-we-do/payroll) handle the details that trip up restaurant owners running payroll themselves:

- Tip credit calculations that keep you compliant with federal minimum wage rules
- Tip reporting and allocation across servers, bartenders, and support staff
- New hire setup and terminations for a workforce that turns over fast
- Multi-location payroll runs on one consistent schedule
- Quarterly and annual payroll tax filings, submitted on time, every time

Restaurant turnover in Utah's service industry runs high, and every new hire or termination is another form, another deadline. We keep the filings current so you're not fielding a penalty notice on top of a Friday night rush.

## Proactive tax planning and preparation for restaurant owners

Most tax preparers meet a restaurant owner once a year, in March, after the decisions that actually affect the tax bill have already been made. Kinexus works differently. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc, build [year-round tax planning](/what-we-do/business-tax) with restaurant clients: claiming the FICA tip credit correctly, timing equipment purchases and build-out depreciation to match cash flow, and revisiting entity structure as a single location grows into a multi-unit operation. A restaurant that buys a new walk-in freezer in October needs that conversation in October, not the following April. That's how a St. George restaurant owner keeps more of what the business earns instead of writing a surprise check to the IRS every spring.
