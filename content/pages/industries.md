## A CPA firm that knows your industry, not just your numbers

Generic tax prep treats every business the same: same forms, same deadlines, same generic advice. A contractor tracking WIP schedules and retainage has different needs than a physician structuring a practice or a restaurant owner watching food cost percentages and tipped payroll. Kinexus CPAs & Advisors builds its approach around six sectors we know from the ground up: construction, restaurants, real estate investing, medical practices, entrepreneurship, and service-based businesses. Southern Utah's economy runs on these industries, and each one carries its own tax rules, cash flow patterns, and compliance risks. Instead of filing a return once a year and calling it done, our CPAs and advisors stay engaged year-round, watching for the deductions, entity moves, and planning windows specific to your business. Below is how that expertise applies to yours.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: construction-site-project-review.jpg | alt: "Contractor and accountant reviewing blueprints on a construction site" | query: "contractor reviewing blueprints construction site" -->
## Construction and contractors

Construction accounting isn't like retail or professional-services accounting. Job costing has to tie back to actual project performance, work-in-progress (WIP) reporting has to satisfy bonding companies and lenders, and cash flow gets squeezed by retainage held until a job closes out. Kinexus works with contractors to build WIP schedules that reflect percentage-of-completion accurately, so your financials match what's actually happening on the jobsite. We plan around equipment purchases, multi-state jobs, and labor classification, looking for the deduction strategies that keep more revenue in your pocket instead of a quarterly surprise. Explore our [construction accounting services](/who-we-serve/construction) or see how [WIP schedules](/industries/construction/wip-schedules-percentage-completion) affect your bonding capacity.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: restaurant-owner-reviewing-finances.jpg | alt: "Restaurant owner reviewing financial reports in a kitchen office" | query: "restaurant owner reviewing financial paperwork" -->
## Restaurants and food & beverage

Restaurants run on thin margins, and a few percentage points in food cost or labor can turn a profitable quarter into a break-even one. Payroll gets complicated fast with tipped employees, multiple locations, and shifting minimum wage rules, and inventory has to be tracked closely enough to catch shrinkage before it eats into profit. Kinexus treats restaurant clients as an ongoing operating partnership, not a once-a-year filing relationship. We review labor cost percentages, help structure multi-location tax strategy, and keep payroll compliant across every location you run. Learn more about our [restaurant accounting services](/who-we-serve/restaurants) or how we handle [multi-location and franchise tax strategy](/industries/restaurants/multi-location-franchise-tax-strategy).

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: real-estate-investor-property-review.jpg | alt: "Real estate investor reviewing rental property documents with advisor" | query: "real estate investor reviewing property documents" -->
## Real estate investors and rental property owners

Real estate investors need more than someone to file a Schedule E. Depreciation strategy, cost segregation, and 1031 exchange timing all affect how much of your rental income actually stays taxable, and how you hold a property changes both your liability exposure and your tax bill. Passive activity rules add another layer that trips up investors who don't plan ahead. Kinexus works with rental property owners and investors year-round, not just at filing season, to structure entities correctly and time transactions for maximum benefit. Whether you own a handful of rentals or a growing portfolio, see our [real estate investor services](/who-we-serve/real-estate-investors) and [real estate tax planning](/who-we-serve/real-estate).

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: medical-practice-owner-consultation.jpg | alt: "Physician reviewing practice financial statements with an advisor" | query: "physician reviewing financial statements office" -->
## Medical and healthcare professionals

Physicians, dentists, and healthcare providers face a specific challenge: running a business while staying focused on patient care. Entity structuring affects liability protection and how much you pay in self-employment tax, and retirement plan design, whether a SEP IRA, solo 401(k), or defined benefit plan, can shelter significant income when it's set up correctly. Add payroll for clinical and administrative staff, equipment purchases, and practice buy-in or buy-out transactions, and the accounting workload grows fast. Kinexus works with medical professionals to handle the business side so providers can stay focused on patients. See our [medical practice accounting services](/who-we-serve/medical-professionals) for specifics.

## Entrepreneurs and service-based businesses

Every business starts somewhere, and the decisions made in year one, entity type, accounting method, payroll setup, follow a company for years. Our [Business Foundation Services](/what-we-do/business-foundation-services) guide entrepreneurs through those early choices before they turn into expensive mistakes. As a consulting firm, agency, coaching practice, or IT staffing business grows past the founder-does-everything stage, the need shifts toward CFO-level insight: cash flow forecasting, margin analysis, and hiring decisions backed by real numbers. Our [Business Management Services](/what-we-do/business-management-services) fill that gap without the cost of a full-time CFO. Whether you're launching or scaling, see how we support [entrepreneurs](/who-we-serve/entrepreneurs) and [service-based businesses](/who-we-serve/service-based-businesses).
