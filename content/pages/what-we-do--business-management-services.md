## A CFO-level partner for growing businesses

Bookkeeping tells you what happened last month. A CFO-level advisor tells you what to do next. Kinexus Business Management Services translate financial statements into decisions: when to hire, when to buy equipment, when to raise prices, and when to hold cash instead of spending it.

Most bookkeeping shops and directory-listed providers around St. George stop at monthly reconciliations. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc, built this service for owners who need more than a general ledger: a strategist who understands construction job costing, restaurant food and labor margins, or a medical practice's payer mix.

This is advisory work led by CPAs with master's-level accounting training, delivered on a schedule that matches how fast your business actually moves.

## What our business management services include

Business Management Services cover the ground between bookkeeping and true financial strategy. It's built for owners who want a clear read on where the business stands and a plan for where it's headed next.

- Cash flow forecasting so you know what's coming before it hits your account
- KPI reporting and dashboards tailored to your industry's real performance drivers
- Budgeting and variance analysis that flags problems before they compound
- Quarterly financial strategy sessions with a CPA, not a generic account manager
- Entity structuring guidance for tax efficiency and liability protection, detailed further on our [entity structuring page](/services/entity-structuring)
- Operations efficiency consulting drawing on our team's background in operations management, covered in depth under [operations efficiency consulting](/services/operations-efficiency-consulting)

Each engagement is scoped to the business, not sold as a fixed package. A single-location restaurant needs different reporting than a contractor running three active job sites.

## Built for growing and multi-entity businesses

Growth creates complexity fast. A contractor bidding bigger jobs, a real estate investor adding a third rental LLC, a consultant spinning off a second brand: each of these moves changes how the business should be structured and reported.

Our team, including Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc, works with entrepreneurs and investors who are past the single-entity stage and need someone tracking consolidated performance across entities, not just filing separate returns for each one.

That means holding companies, management fees between related entities, and inter-entity loans get structured correctly from the start, with guidance detailed on our [entity structuring services page](/services/entity-structuring). Multi-entity businesses that skip this step usually pay for it later in cleanup costs and missed deductions.

## Proactive strategy, not year-end surprises

A lot of firms show up in March, prepare a return, and disappear until next year. That model works fine for simple returns. It fails growing businesses, where a decision made in June, a new hire, a lease, an equipment purchase, changes what April looks like.

Business Management Services run on a year-round rhythm: regular check-ins, updated forecasts, and a standing conversation about what's changing in the business. It pairs directly with our [advanced tax planning](/services/advanced-tax-planning) work for owners in higher income brackets, so tax strategy and operational strategy move together instead of getting reconciled after the fact.

The result is fewer surprises at tax time and more decisions made with real numbers instead of a gut check.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Business management services

**Q: What are business management services?**
A: Business management services are CFO-style advisory services that go beyond bookkeeping, covering cash flow forecasting, KPI reporting, budgeting, financial strategy sessions, and entity structuring.
