## Stay ahead with proactive tax and financial strategy

Most CPA firms show up once a year, hand you a return, and disappear until next April. Kinexus CPAs works differently. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and the rest of the team meet with clients throughout the year, because a tax strategy decided in March is worth more than one decided in April.

That's the thread running through this archive. A WIP schedule review in June affects your bonding capacity in December. An entity structure conversation before you sign a lease changes your tax bill for years. Wait until the deadline, and the best options are already off the table.

Construction firms, restaurant groups, real estate investors, medical practices, and service businesses across St George, UT face the same problem: money moves faster than annual meetings can track. That's why this archive exists, and why Kinexus builds client relationships around ongoing planning instead of one appointment a year.
