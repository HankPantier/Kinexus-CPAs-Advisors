## A financial partner built for consultants, agencies, coaches, and staffing firms

Consultants, marketing agencies, IT firms, staffing companies, and coaches run on a different financial model than a retail shop or a contractor. Revenue comes in retainers, project fees, or placement commissions. Payroll might blend W-2 staff with a bench of 1099 contractors. Growth often outpaces the founder's ability to track it all in a spreadsheet.

Kinexus CPAs & Advisors works with service-based businesses across St. George, Utah, who need more than a bookkeeper who logs transactions once a month. Our team, led by CPAs Shawn T. Gubler (CTC, MAcc), RJ Mathews, Chase Gubler (MAcc), and Morgan Nethercott, alongside EAs Andy Stewart and Peter Williams (MAcc), builds financial systems around how consulting, agency, coaching, and staffing businesses actually operate. That's what a dedicated partner for business growth and wealth looks like, not just a filer of returns.

## The financial challenges service businesses face as they scale

Service businesses hit predictable friction points as they grow past the founder's laptop and personal spreadsheet.

- Revenue that's lumpy. Retainers renew, project fees land in chunks, and staffing placements pay out on client timelines you don't control, which makes forecasting hard without a system built for irregular cash flow.
- Contractor versus employee classification. A firm mixing 1099 subcontractors with W-2 staff faces payroll tax exposure and IRS scrutiny the moment those lines blur.
- Founder-led bottlenecks. The person who built the business is often still approving invoices and tracking billable hours, which caps growth at the limit of one calendar.
- Billing-cycle cash gaps. Net-30 and net-60 client terms mean payroll and rent come due long before invoices clear, squeezing working capital right when you're hiring.

These show up in the books of agencies, consultancies, and staffing firms month after month. They're solvable with the right structure in place before they become a crisis.

## CFO-level guidance to turn financial data into growth decisions

At some point, bookkeeping stops being the bottleneck and decisions become the bottleneck: what to charge, who to hire next, whether to spin up a second entity for a new service line. That's where Business Management Services come in. It's CFO-style advisory, not just monthly reports, translating your numbers into a position on pricing, staffing, and cash reserves.

Shawn T. Gubler holds the Certified Tax Coach (CTC) credential, which means tax strategy for growing agencies and consultancies gets built around your specific structure, not a generic checklist. For founders juggling multiple LLCs, S-corp elections, or a staffing operation expanding into new states, that level of planning is the difference between reacting to a tax bill and shaping it months in advance.

## A modern, secure process built around how you work

Consultants travel. Agency teams work from client sites. Coaches run their business from a phone between sessions. A firm that still wants paper folders and in-person drop-offs doesn't fit that pace, and it shouldn't be a requirement for good accounting.

Documents move through a secure digital portal, not email attachments or a filing cabinet. Payroll approvals, monthly financials, and tax documents are available wherever your team happens to be that week. It's built for people who run distributed, client-facing businesses and don't have time to chase down a signature in person.
