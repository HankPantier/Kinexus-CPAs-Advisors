## Practical guidance for growing businesses and busy owners

Most accounting blogs recycle the same generic tips you could find anywhere. This one doesn't. Every article, alert, and short video on this page comes from the CPAs, CTC, and EAs who actually prepare returns, run payroll, and build tax strategies for contractors, restaurant owners, real estate investors, and medical practices in St. George, UT.

Shawn Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and the rest of the Kinexus team write from what they see in client meetings each week, not from a content calendar. That means fewer listicles and more of the specific guidance business owners actually ask for: how a WIP schedule affects bonding capacity, when an S-corp election stops making sense, what triggers an IRS notice. New insights post regularly, and the team behind them is the same one you'd call for a consultation.
