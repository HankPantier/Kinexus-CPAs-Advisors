## A dedicated financial partner for growing businesses

Most accounting firms show up once a year, hand you a tax bill, and go quiet until next April. That model doesn't work for a contractor managing job costs, a restaurant owner juggling labor margins, or a physician trying to build wealth outside the practice. Kinexus CPAs & Advisors was built around year-round advisory work instead: accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, tax planning, and CFO-level guidance delivered through a modern, secure digital process. St. George, UT business owners, real estate investors, and entrepreneurs get a firm that answers the phone in July, not just March. Every service below connects to a bigger plan for growth, not a once-a-year transaction.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: outsourced-accounting-bookkeeping.jpg | alt: "Bookkeeper reviewing financial statements on a laptop in an office" | query: "bookkeeper reviewing financial reports laptop" -->
## Outsourced accounting and bookkeeping built for growth

Data entry shouldn't be the reason a business owner works nights. [Outsourced accounting](/what-we-do/outsourced-accounting) from Kinexus covers the full back office: financial statements, reconciliations, accounts payable, and reporting that actually tells you something useful about the business. [Bookkeeping](/what-we-do/bookkeeping) services handle the routine work, transaction classification, expense tracking, bill payment, and invoicing, so the books stay current instead of becoming a scramble at tax time.

Everything runs through a secure digital platform built for document exchange and real-time visibility, which matters whether the client is a single-location restaurant or a multi-property real estate investor. Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc, and the accounting team structure the process around each client's industry, not a generic template. Contractors get job-cost visibility. Service businesses get clean recurring-revenue reporting. The result is books an owner can actually use to make decisions.

## Payroll processing that keeps your team paid accurately and on time

Payroll mistakes cost more than money. A missed filing deadline or a misclassified employee turns into a compliance headache fast. Kinexus [payroll](/what-we-do/payroll) services cover employee setup, salary and wage changes, garnishments, and the ongoing tax filings that keep a business in good standing with state and federal agencies.

- Accurate, on-time processing for hourly, salaried, and mixed teams
- New hire setup and employee status changes handled without delay
- State and federal payroll tax filings managed on schedule
- Direct coordination with bookkeeping so payroll data flows straight into the books
- One point of contact instead of a call center

## Proactive business and personal tax planning, not just filing

Filing a tax return reports what already happened. Planning changes what happens next. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, holds the Certified Tax Coach designation, which centers on the kind of advanced, legal tax-reduction strategy that most once-a-year preparers never bring up: entity structure, retirement plan design, depreciation timing, and income shifting for owners in higher brackets.

[Business tax](/what-we-do/business-tax) work at Kinexus runs year-round, with quarterly check-ins that catch opportunities before December instead of after. [Personal income tax](/what-we-do/personal-income-tax) planning follows the same approach for owners whose personal and business finances are tightly linked, which describes most contractors, physicians, and real estate investors. RJ Mathews, CPA, and Morgan Nethercott, CPA, round out a team that treats the return as the last step in a longer strategy, not the whole engagement. For owners ready to go further, [advanced tax planning](/services/advanced-tax-planning) and [entity structuring](/services/entity-structuring) dig into the specifics.

## IRS audit protection and tax resolution support

An IRS letter shows up and most business owners assume the worst. It usually doesn't have to be. [Audit protection](/what-we-do/audit-protection) at Kinexus means representation and support the moment a notice arrives, whether it's a routine document request or a full examination.

Andrew (Andy) James Stewart, EA, and Peter Williams, EA, MAcc, hold Enrolled Agent credentials, which grant them the authority to represent clients directly before the IRS on notices, audits, and back-tax matters. That distinction matters for a construction business facing a payroll tax inquiry or a real estate investor being questioned on depreciation schedules. Instead of navigating IRS correspondence alone, clients hand it to someone licensed to speak for them. For back-tax balances or ongoing collection issues, [IRS representation and tax resolution](/services/irs-representation-tax-resolution) covers negotiation and resolution from start to finish.
