## Latest articles from our advisory team

This is page 4 of the Kinexus CPAs & Advisors insights library, where our St. George team breaks down the tax and accounting questions that come up most in client meetings. If you've already flipped through pages 1 through 3, you'll find a different set of topics here, ranging from construction job costing to nonprofit fraud controls to the tax mechanics of selling a business. New posts get added regularly, so bookmark this page if you're working through the archive in order.

### Reading a WIP schedule the way your banker does
photo: construction-wip-schedule-review.jpg
query: contractor reviewing financial documents office

A percentage-of-completion schedule tells a lender more about your construction business than your bank balance does. Here's what they're actually looking for and how to keep the numbers clean between draws. [Read more](/what-a-wip-schedule-actually-tells-you-and-why-your-banker-cares)

### Retainage and the cash flow squeeze contractors know too well
photo: contractor-invoice-retainage.jpg
query: construction paperwork invoice review

Retainage protects the project owner, but it can leave a contractor short on cash for months at a time. A few planning moves can soften the impact before it hits your operating account. [Read more](/retainage-is-holding-your-cash-hostage-heres-how-to-plan-around-it)

### Building fraud controls into a nonprofit's daily routine
photo: nonprofit-finance-team-meeting.jpg
query: nonprofit staff reviewing budget documents

Small nonprofits often run lean, which makes segregation of duties harder to enforce. This piece covers practical controls that fit a two- or three-person finance team. [Read more](/fraud-prevention-strategies-for-nonprofit-organizations)

### Selling a business, rental, or large asset this year
photo: business-owner-signing-documents.jpg
query: business owner signing contract meeting

A sale that closes before you've talked to your CPA usually costs more in taxes than one that's planned in advance. Here's the call to make before you sign anything. [Read more](/selling-a-business-rental-or-large-asset-this-year-call-your-cpa-before-you-sign)

### Why a business valuation matters before you think you need one
photo: financial-analyst-calculating-valuation.jpg
query: financial analyst reviewing charts calculator

Buy-sell agreements, succession plans, and even divorce proceedings all hinge on a defensible number. Waiting until you need a valuation urgently rarely produces the best outcome. [Read more](/when-and-why-every-business-owner-needs-a-business-valuation)

### Transfer pricing basics for owners with related entities
photo: business-owner-reviewing-entity-structure.jpg
query: business owner reviewing paperwork desk

If you run more than one entity and they transact with each other, the IRS expects those prices to reflect market terms. This overview explains where owners commonly get exposed. [Read more](/transfer-pricing-and-related-entities-what-business-owners-need-to-know)

## Popular topics in tax planning and business advisory

A handful of themes keep showing up across this archive, and for good reason. Business owners in Washington County are asking the same core questions: is my entity structure still the right one, is my back office keeping pace with growth, and am I planning for taxes year-round instead of scrambling every April.

Entity structuring is one of the most-read topics here, and it's easy to see why. A structure that made sense at $200,000 in revenue can cost real money at $2 million. Our piece on [whether your business structure is costing you money](/is-your-business-structure-costing-you-money) walks through the warning signs.

Operational efficiency is the other recurring thread. Owners want fewer surprises in their books and a clearer read on margin. Articles on [zero-based budgeting during persistent inflation](/zero-based-budgeting-a-smarter-way-to-manage-your-money-during-persistent-inflation) and [upskilling a workforce facing labor shortages](/upskilling-your-workforce-a-smart-strategy-for-overcoming-labor-market-shortages) both tackle that from different angles.
