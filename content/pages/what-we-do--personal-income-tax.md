## A personal tax partner who plans ahead, not just files

Most tax preparers in St. George see you once a year, in a rush, right before the April deadline. That's not how Kinexus CPAs & Advisors works. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and the rest of our team treat personal tax preparation as a year-round conversation, not a once-a-year transaction.

A Certified Tax Coach (CTC) designation means proactive planning is built into how we think, not something we bolt on in March. We look at your income, your business, your investments, and your life changes as they happen, so decisions get made when they can still save you money. Filing a return is the last step in that process, not the whole job.

If you're used to a preparer who disappears after tax season, expect something different here.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: business-owner-tax-review.jpg | alt: "Business owner reviewing personal tax documents with a CPA advisor" | query: "business owner meeting accountant office" -->
## Personal tax support built for business owners and professionals

Your personal return rarely stands alone. If you own a construction company, run a restaurant, hold rental property, or practice medicine in Washington County, your 1040 is tied to K-1s, multi-entity income, depreciation schedules, and business decisions made months earlier.

Our team handles both sides of that equation. RJ Mathews, CPA, and Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc, work with clients whose personal and business tax pictures overlap constantly, entrepreneurs drawing income from an S-corp, real estate investors managing passive losses across multiple properties, physicians with practice ownership stakes. Peter Williams, EA, MAcc, brings the same lens to clients navigating complex investment income and multi-state filings.

Because we prepare the business return and the personal return under one roof, nothing gets lost in translation between a client's CPA and their bookkeeper. Estimated payments, entity elections, and personal deductions get planned together, not reconciled after the fact.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: tax-documents-checklist.jpg | alt: "Folder of organized personal tax documents and receipts on a desk" | query: "tax documents folder organized desk" -->
## What's included in your personal tax preparation

A Kinexus personal return goes further than data entry. Here's what's covered:

- Full federal and state income tax preparation, including complex returns with K-1s, rental schedules, and investment income
- Deduction and credit review specific to your situation, not a generic checklist
- Quarterly estimated payment planning so you're not surprised by a balance due
- Coordination with your business tax filings when you own an S-corp, partnership, or rental portfolio
- Secure digital document delivery and e-signature, no printed stacks of paper to track down
- A planning conversation before filing season, not just a form to sign

Every return is reviewed by a credentialed preparer, CPA or EA, before it goes out the door. That review catches the deduction you'd have missed and the estimated payment that would have triggered a penalty next spring.

## A modern, secure process from document drop-off to filing

Many firms in St. George still run on dropped-off folders and in-person appointments. Ours doesn't have to.

### Upload your documents securely
Send W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, and receipts through our encrypted client portal from your phone or laptop, on your schedule.

### Review and planning call
Your preparer walks through the return, flags questions, and talks through any planning opportunities before anything is filed.

### E-sign and file
Approve your return electronically. We file with the IRS and Utah State Tax Commission and confirm acceptance.

### Digital delivery and storage
Your completed return is stored securely in your portal, ready whenever you need it for a mortgage application or next year's planning.

## Support if the IRS comes calling

A notice from the IRS doesn't mean panic, but it does mean you need someone who knows how to respond correctly and quickly. Andrew (Andy) James Stewart, EA, and Peter Williams, EA, MAcc, hold Enrolled Agent credentials, giving them unrestricted rights to represent clients directly before the IRS.

If you receive an audit notice, a balance-due letter, or a request for documentation, our audit protection service steps in to handle correspondence, prepare responses, and represent you in discussions with the IRS. That's a level of backup most local tax preparers don't offer once your return is filed.

Clients working with us on personal returns get that same protection built into the relationship, not sold as a separate emergency service after something has already gone wrong.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Personal income tax

**Q: Do you prepare personal tax returns for business owners with K-1 income?**
A: Yes. Kinexus regularly prepares personal returns that include K-1s from S-corps, partnerships, and rental properties. Because we often prepare the related business return too, personal and business filings are coordinated rather than handled in isolation, which reduces errors and missed deductions.

**Q: How is Kinexus different from a seasonal tax prep shop in St. George?**
A: Kinexus provides year-round planning through CPAs, an Enrolled Agent, and a Certified Tax Coach, not just once-a-year filing. Estimated payments, entity decisions, and deductions get planned before year-end, so filing season holds fewer surprises.

**Q: What happens if I get an IRS notice after my return is filed?**
A: Our audit protection service, backed by Enrolled Agents Andrew Stewart and Peter Williams, handles IRS correspondence and represents you directly. You're not left to interpret a notice alone or hire separate representation after the fact.

**Q: Is the document submission process fully digital?**
A: Yes. Documents are uploaded through a secure client portal, returns are reviewed on a call, and everything is signed and filed electronically. Completed returns are stored digitally, so there's no paper trail to manage or lose.
