## Payroll built for St. George business owners, not spreadsheets

Payroll sounds simple until a subcontractor gets misclassified, a tipped server's W-2 doesn't match the state filing, or your fifth new hire this quarter throws off your labor budget. Most payroll providers process numbers and move on. Kinexus CPAs & Advisors treats payroll as one piece of a bigger financial picture, reviewed by the same CPAs and Enrolled Agents who prepare your tax return and manage your books.

That matters in St. George, where construction crews, restaurant staff, and medical practice teams all have different pay structures, tax exposure, and compliance risks. You won't get routed to a national call center or left alone in a software portal. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and the Kinexus team run payroll as part of a year-round advisory relationship, not a once-a-pay-period transaction.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: payroll-processing-review.jpg | alt: "Accountant reviewing payroll records and pay stubs at a desk" | query: "accountant reviewing payroll documents office" -->
## What our payroll processing includes

Every payroll run at Kinexus covers the full cycle, not just the paycheck. That includes:

- New employee setup, including I-9 verification, W-4 collection, and direct deposit enrollment
- Salary and wage changes processed the same week they're requested, not batched for next cycle
- Timely, accurate processing on your schedule, whether that's weekly, biweekly, or semi-monthly
- Federal and state payroll tax filings prepared and submitted by CPAs and Enrolled Agents, not automated software alone
- Year-end W-2 and 1099 preparation and distribution
- Garnishments, benefits deductions, and PTO tracking handled without extra spreadsheets on your end

Owners in construction and restaurants tell us the same thing: they don't want to learn payroll software, they want it handled correctly and explained plainly when a question comes up.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## Compliance and filings handled for you

Payroll tax mistakes are expensive. A missed federal deposit deadline, an incorrect Utah withholding filing, or a late Department of Workforce Services report can trigger penalties that compound quickly. Kinexus manages federal payroll tax deposits and Form 941 filings, Utah state withholding and unemployment insurance reporting, and local filing requirements specific to Washington County businesses.

Restaurant operators face additional exposure around tip reporting and FICA tip credits. Construction companies juggle prevailing wage requirements on public jobs and worker classification questions between employees and subcontractors. Medical practices manage multi-provider payroll with varying compensation models. Peter Williams, EA, MAcc, and Andrew (Andy) James Stewart, EA, work directly with the IRS and Utah State Tax Commission on your behalf when a notice does arrive, so a filing question never becomes your problem to solve alone.

## Payroll that connects to your bigger financial picture

Payroll run in isolation tells you almost nothing. Payroll run alongside your bookkeeping and outsourced accounting tells you exactly what labor is costing you against revenue, job, or location, in real time.

Kinexus builds payroll data directly into the financial reporting your CPA already reviews. That means labor cost percentages show up in your monthly financials without a separate reconciliation step, and a payroll change flows into your budget the same week it happens. RJ Mathews, CPA, and the business management services team use that connected data to flag labor cost trends before they erode margin, whether you're staffing a second restaurant location or scaling a service firm past ten employees. Payroll stops being a standalone chore and becomes another input your CPA is already watching.

## Industry-specific payroll challenges we solve

### Construction and Trades
icon: Hammer

Crew-based pay, prevailing wage jobs, and the constant question of employee versus 1099 subcontractor create real audit exposure. Kinexus helps classify workers correctly and keeps job-cost payroll data tied to your WIP schedules.

### Restaurants and Food Service
icon: Receipt

Tipped wages, shift differentials, and high turnover mean payroll changes weekly, sometimes daily. We handle tip credit calculations and multi-location staffing so a manager change at one restaurant doesn't create a filing error at another.

### Medical Practices
icon: Stethoscope

Physicians, associates, and hygienists often sit on different compensation structures within the same practice. Kinexus manages varied pay schedules, benefits deductions, and bonus structures without slowing down your front office.

### Service-Based Businesses
icon: Briefcase

Consultants, agencies, and staffing firms scaling past a handful of employees need payroll that keeps pace with hiring. We set up new team members fast and keep multi-state or remote-worker filings straight as you grow.

## A modern, secure payroll experience

Onboarding a new hire shouldn't mean printing forms, scanning them, and emailing PDFs back and forth. Kinexus runs payroll through a secure digital portal where employees complete their own onboarding paperwork, update direct deposit details, and access pay stubs without a phone call to your office.

On your end, approving a payroll run, reviewing a report, or signing a filing takes minutes from a phone or laptop. Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc, and Morgan Nethercott, CPA, oversee the same digital process used across bookkeeping and outsourced accounting engagements, so your payroll data lives in one secure system instead of scattered spreadsheets and email threads.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Payroll

**Q: Does Kinexus handle payroll tax filings for St. George businesses?**
A: Yes. Kinexus prepares and files federal payroll tax deposits, Form 941s, Utah state withholding, and unemployment insurance reports. CPAs and Enrolled Agents on staff also handle any resulting IRS or state notices.

**Q: Can Kinexus manage payroll for tipped employees at a restaurant?**
A: Yes. Kinexus calculates tip credits, tracks tipped versus non-tipped hours, and handles multi-location staffing for restaurant clients, keeping tip reporting accurate across every shift and location.

**Q: How does Kinexus tell employees from 1099 subcontractors on a construction crew?**
A: Kinexus reviews work arrangements against IRS and Utah classification standards to reduce audit risk, then sets up payroll or 1099 reporting correctly from the start rather than correcting it after a filing deadline.

**Q: Is payroll data connected to my other financial reports?**
A: Yes. Payroll flows directly into the bookkeeping and financial reporting Kinexus already manages, giving you real-time labor cost percentages instead of a separate spreadsheet reconciled after the fact.
