## Challenges unique to manufacturing and distribution businesses

Manufacturing and distribution carry accounting complexities that trip up firms accustomed to simpler service businesses.

- **Inventory costing:** FIFO, LIFO, or weighted average methods change your reported margins and your tax bill. Get it wrong and you either overpay taxes or misstate profitability to your bank.
- **Supply chain cash flow gaps:** Raw materials get purchased months before finished goods sell. Without a forecast built around your production cycle, payroll and supplier payments collide with slow-paying customers.
- **Equipment and capital investment decisions:** A new CNC machine, forklift fleet, or warehouse expansion changes your depreciation schedule, loan covenants, and tax position all at once.
- **Margin pressure:** Rising material costs and freight rates squeeze distributors from both ends. Job costing that is even a few weeks stale hides which product lines are actually profitable.

Firms outside this niche often lump these numbers into generic categories that satisfy a tax return but tell you nothing about how to run the business.
