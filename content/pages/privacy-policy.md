## Our commitment to your privacy

Kinexus CPAs & Advisors works with tax returns, payroll records, bank statements, and other sensitive financial documents every day. That kind of access comes with responsibility, and we treat it accordingly. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and client portal, why we collect it, and the specific steps we take to keep it secure. If a section here doesn't answer your question, our St George, UT team is a phone call or email away.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## Information we collect

When you visit stgcpas.com or use our secure client portal, we may collect a few categories of information. Contact form submissions capture your name, email, phone number, and any details you share about your business or tax situation. Document uploads through our client portal can include financial statements, W-2s, 1099s, bank records, and other files needed to prepare a return or manage your books. Our website also uses standard analytics cookies to track page visits and general browsing behavior, which helps us understand what visitors are looking for and improve site navigation and content over time.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## How we use your information

We use the information you provide to deliver the services you've engaged us for: tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll processing, business advisory, and audit representation among them. Client data is used to prepare accurate filings, respond to IRS or state agency correspondence, and communicate updates about your account. Website analytics help us refine content and identify which pages and industry resources visitors find most useful. In every case, data use ties back to a service you've requested or a legal and regulatory obligation, such as IRS recordkeeping requirements, that we're bound to follow.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## How we protect your data

Financial documents move through an encrypted client portal rather than email attachments, reducing the exposure that comes with sending sensitive files over unsecured channels. Portal access requires individual client login credentials, and internal systems are restricted to staff who need the information to complete your work. Our team, including CPAs Shawn T. Gubler, RJ Mathews, Chase Gubler, and Morgan Nethercott, along with EAs Andrew Stewart and Peter Williams, follows firm data-handling protocols consistent with AICPA guidance on client confidentiality. No system is invulnerable, but our digital process is built specifically to limit who can see your data and how it travels.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## Sharing and disclosure of information

Kinexus CPAs & Advisors does not sell client or website visitor information. Data is shared only when necessary to complete the services you've hired us for, for example, transmitting a return to the IRS, submitting payroll filings to a state agency, or coordinating with a payroll processor on your behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law, such as in response to a subpoena or regulatory audit request. Any third-party service providers we use to support portal hosting or payroll processing are bound by their own confidentiality and security obligations.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## Your rights and choices

You can ask us at any time to review, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to any records we're legally required to retain, such as IRS-mandated tax records. If you'd rather not receive marketing emails or newsletters from us, every message includes an unsubscribe option, and you can also let us know directly. Most browsers let you disable or limit cookies through your settings if you prefer not to be tracked by website analytics tools.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## Contact us about this policy

Questions about how Kinexus CPAs & Advisors collects, uses, or protects your information are welcome anytime. Reach out to our St George, UT office and a member of our team will get back to you directly, no runaround. If you're ready to talk through your accounting, tax, or advisory needs with a CPA who already understands the questions you're asking, [schedule a consultation](/contact) and we'll take it from there.

<!-- block: faq-accordion -->
## Frequently Asked Questions About Privacy policy

**Q: Does Kinexus CPAs & Advisors sell my personal information?**
A: No. Kinexus CPAs & Advisors does not sell client or website visitor data to third parties. Information is used only to deliver the accounting, tax, payroll, or advisory services you've requested, or to meet legal and regulatory obligations such as IRS filing requirements.

**Q: How are my financial documents kept secure?**
A: Documents move through an encrypted client portal rather than email attachments. Access is limited to individual client login credentials and to staff members who need the information to complete your engagement, following firm protocols consistent with AICPA confidentiality guidance.

**Q: Can I request that my information be deleted?**
A: Yes, you can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information at any time. Some financial records may need to be retained longer to comply with IRS recordkeeping rules, but we'll explain any limits that apply to your specific request.

**Q: Does the Kinexus website use cookies?**
A: Yes, the site uses standard analytics cookies to understand page visits and browsing patterns, which helps improve site content and navigation. You can disable or limit cookies through your browser settings at any time.
