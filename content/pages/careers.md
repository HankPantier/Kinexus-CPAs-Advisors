## Build your accounting career with a growing St. George firm

Most accounting jobs promise variety and deliver the same 1040s and bank reconciliations on repeat. Kinexus CPAs & Advisors is built differently. We work with contractors, restaurant owners, real estate investors, medical practices, and service-based businesses across Washington County and beyond, which means the work in front of you changes by the client, not just by the season.

We run on a modern, secure digital process for document handling and filing, so you're not buried in paper or chasing spreadsheets across five different systems. And because we do year-round proactive tax planning and advisory work instead of once-a-year tax prep, the job doesn't shut off in May and restart in January. If you want a career that builds real technical depth, not just a seat that fills chairs during tax season, this is where that happens.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: kinexus-team-culture.jpg | alt: "Accounting team members collaborating around a desk in a bright office" | query: "office team collaboration professionals" -->
## What makes working at Kinexus different

We've built a team that sticks around. Long employee tenure at Kinexus isn't an accident; it comes from giving people meaningful client relationships instead of assembly-line compliance work. A few things set the day-to-day apart:

- Year-round advisory work with construction, restaurant, real estate, and medical practice clients, not a tax-season sprint followed by slow months
- Direct exposure to CFO-level business management services, so staff learn strategy alongside compliance
- A modern digital workflow for document collection and filing that cuts down on manual paper-pushing
- A team culture where specialists like Leslye Haws, our Bookkeeping Specialist, build long careers rather than short stints
- Industry-specific client rosters that let you develop real expertise instead of generalist knowledge spread thin

## Roles we regularly hire for

Candidates come to Kinexus from a range of backgrounds. Here's where most people land first.

### Bookkeeping Specialist
icon: Receipt

Handles transaction classification, expense tracking, invoicing, and bill payment for clients across construction, restaurants, and service businesses. Leslye Haws started here and built a specialized career around it.

### Staff Accountant
icon: Calculator

Supports outsourced accounting engagements, reconciliations, and financial reporting for small business clients, with a path toward CPA licensure and advisory work.

### Payroll Specialist
icon: Users

Manages payroll processing, employee setup, salary changes, and filings for a portfolio of business clients across multiple industries.

### Tax Preparer
icon: FileText

Prepares business and personal returns under the guidance of CPAs and EAs like Shawn T. Gubler and Peter Williams, with exposure to proactive tax planning, not just filing.

### CFO Advisory Staff
icon: ChartLine

Works alongside senior advisors on business management services, turning financial statements into decisions for contractors, medical practices, and growing entrepreneurs.

## Growth, mentorship, and credential support

Our leadership team holds real, specific credentials: Shawn T. Gubler carries CPA, CTC, and MAcc designations; RJ Mathews and Chase Gubler are CPAs; Andy Stewart and Peter Williams are Enrolled Agents; Morgan Nethercott is a CPA. That mix exists because we invest in staff earning it, not just hiring it.

If you're working toward a credential, expect support that goes beyond a mention in your review:

- Structured mentorship from CPAs and EAs on live client work, not hypothetical case studies
- Support pursuing EA, CPA, or Certified Tax Coach (CTC) designations
- Direct exposure to IRS representation, audit protection, and tax resolution work
- Involvement in advanced tax planning and entity structuring conversations most firms reserve for partners
- A track for moving from bookkeeping or staff accounting into CFO-level advisory roles

## Life in St. George and at Kinexus

St. George offers year-round outdoor access, a growing small business economy, and a lower cost of living than most Utah metro areas, without giving up career opportunity. Kinexus reflects that same balance: a firm serious enough to handle CFO-level advisory work for construction and medical clients, but small enough that your name and your growth actually get tracked.

We hire people who want both things. If Utah's Dixie is home, or you're looking to make it home, the work here is built to match the life.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Careers

**Q: What roles does Kinexus CPAs hire for in St. George, UT?**
A: Kinexus regularly hires bookkeeping specialists, staff accountants, payroll specialists, tax preparers, and CFO advisory staff. Roles support clients in construction, restaurants, real estate, and medical practices, with a mix of compliance work and year-round advisory projects.

**Q: Does Kinexus support staff pursuing CPA or EA credentials?**
A: Yes. Kinexus provides mentorship from credentialed CPAs and EAs, including Shawn T. Gubler (CPA, CTC, MAcc) and Peter Williams (EA, MAcc), and supports staff working toward CPA, EA, or Certified Tax Coach designations through hands-on client work.

**Q: Is tax season the busiest time to work at Kinexus?**
A: Not exclusively. Kinexus runs year-round proactive tax planning and advisory work rather than a once-a-year tax prep model, which spreads client work more evenly across the calendar compared to typical seasonal compliance firms.

**Q: How do I apply for a job at Kinexus CPAs?**
A: Reach out through the firm's contact page to submit a resume or ask about current openings. Kinexus fills specific roles as they open rather than maintaining a constant job board, so direct contact is the fastest path to a real conversation.
