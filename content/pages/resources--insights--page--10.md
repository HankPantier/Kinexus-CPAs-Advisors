## Stay ahead with proactive tax planning strategies

Most CPA firms show up in March. Kinexus works with clients through the whole year, adjusting quarterly estimates, revisiting entity structure, and flagging opportunities before December 31st closes the door. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, leads much of this planning work, applying the Certified Tax Coach designation to look for legal deductions and credits that once-a-year prep tends to miss. If your current filer only calls during tax season, that's a planning gap worth fixing. Our [advanced tax planning](/services/advanced-tax-planning) services and [business structure](/is-your-business-structure-costing-you-money) guidance are a good next stop.

## Get answers to your IRS and tax resolution questions

An IRS letter shows up and suddenly every other priority moves down the list. Our enrolled agents, Andrew (Andy) James Stewart, EA, and Peter Williams, EA, MAcc, handle notices, audits, and back-tax cases directly with the IRS on a client's behalf. That includes correspondence audits, payment plan negotiation, and penalty abatement requests. If you've landed on this archive page while searching for help with a specific notice, skip the back button and go straight to [IRS representation and tax resolution](/services/irs-representation-tax-resolution) or [audit protection](/what-we-do/audit-protection).
