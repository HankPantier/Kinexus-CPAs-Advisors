## Facing an IRS notice or audit does not mean facing it alone

An IRS letter shows up in the mailbox and the rest of the day disappears. Maybe it's an audit notice, a demand for returns you never filed, or a notice that a lien is about to hit your property. The first instinct is panic. The second is not knowing who to call.

Kinexus CPAs & Advisors handles these situations year-round, not just during tax season. Licensed enrolled agents on our team step in as your authorized representative, communicate directly with the IRS on your behalf, and build a resolution plan based on your actual case file rather than guesswork. You don't need to understand tax code to get through this. You need someone who already does, sitting across the table from you in St. George, ready to make the calls the IRS won't return when you place them yourself.

## How our Enrolled Agent credential works in your favor

Not every accountant can legally speak to the IRS on your behalf. An Enrolled Agent, or EA, holds a federal license granting unlimited rights of representation before the IRS, covering audits, collections, and appeals, in any state, for any tax matter. It's a credential earned through IRS examination or prior IRS service, not a title anyone can claim.

Andy Stewart, EA, and Peter Williams, EA, MAcc, hold this license at Kinexus. That means when the IRS wants documentation, a response, or a negotiation, they talk to us directly instead of routing everything back through you. A bookkeeper can help you organize paperwork. An EA can sign a power of attorney, argue your case, and negotiate terms with an IRS agent on the phone. That distinction matters the moment a case gets contested.

## What happens after you contact us

### We pull and review your IRS file
Before we respond to anything, we request a full transcript of your account directly from the IRS. This shows us exactly what they have on record, what deadlines are real, and what options are actually available in your case.

### We build a strategy session around your numbers
You meet with an enrolled agent or CPA to walk through the findings, your goals, and the realistic paths forward, whether that's abatement, an installment agreement, or audit defense.

### We represent you directly with the IRS
Once you sign a power of attorney, we become your point of contact. Calls, letters, and negotiations run through our office in St. George, not yours.

## Frequently Asked Questions About IRS representation and tax resolution

**Q: What does an Enrolled Agent do that a regular accountant cannot?**
A: An Enrolled Agent holds a federal license granting unlimited rights to represent clients before the IRS in audits, collections, and appeals nationwide. Unlike unlicensed preparers or bookkeepers, EAs can sign power of attorney and negotiate directly with IRS agents on your behalf.

**Q: How long does IRS tax resolution take?**
A: Timelines vary by case. A penalty abatement request may resolve in weeks, while an audit or complex back-tax negotiation can take several months. We review your IRS file first, which lets us give you a realistic timeline before any work begins.

**Q: Can Kinexus help if I haven't filed returns in several years?**
A: Yes. We reconstruct missing records, prepare and file the outstanding returns, and negotiate with the IRS to limit penalties tied to the unfiled period. Most clients are surprised at how manageable this becomes once the paperwork is actually organized.

**Q: Will contacting Kinexus stop a wage garnishment or lien?**
A: Contacting us starts the process of intervening directly with the IRS, which can lead to a release or modification of garnishment or lien action. Timing matters significantly here, so earlier contact gives us more negotiating options.

**Q: Do I need to live in St. George to use this service?**
A: No. IRS representation is handled through secure digital document exchange and direct communication with the IRS, so clients throughout Utah and beyond work with our enrolled agents without needing to visit the office in person.
