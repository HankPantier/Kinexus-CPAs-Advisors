## What to do if you've received an audit notice

The first 48 hours after an audit letter arrives matter more than most people realize. Here's the short version of what to do, and what not to do.

### Don't ignore the deadline
IRS and Utah state notices include a response window, usually 30 days. Missing it can escalate the matter or eliminate options you'd otherwise have.

### Don't respond alone
Every statement you make to an examiner becomes part of the record. Route communication through a representative who knows what the agent is actually asking for.

### Gather what you already have
Pull tax returns, bank statements, and any records referenced in the notice. Don't recreate anything or guess at figures.

### Call before you reply
Bring the notice to Kinexus before sending anything back to the IRS. We'll review the specific issue, whether it's a correspondence audit, a document request, or a full examination, and outline next steps within your first conversation.

### Enroll in ongoing representation
Once we're engaged, all further correspondence with the IRS or state agency goes through our office, not yours.
