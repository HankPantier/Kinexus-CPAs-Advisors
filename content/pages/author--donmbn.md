## Get to know Don's role at Kinexus

Don works as a content contributor for Kinexus CPAs & Advisors, the St. George, UT firm known for its tagline "Dedicated Partner for Business Growth & Wealth." His job is straightforward: sit down with the firm's CPAs and enrolled agents, pull out the details that actually matter to a business owner, and write it up in language that doesn't require an accounting degree to follow.

Don works closely with Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and RJ Mathews, CPA, to keep every article current with changing tax law and the real client situations the firm handles across southern Utah. He isn't the one signing tax returns or representing clients before the IRS; that work belongs to the credentialed team at Kinexus. His role is making sure that expertise reaches readers before they ever need to make an appointment.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: content-writer-research-notes.jpg | alt: "Writer reviewing tax documents and notes at a desk" | query: "writer reviewing financial documents desk" -->
## Areas of expertise and industries served

Don's articles track the same niches Kinexus builds its practice around: construction and contracting, restaurants and food and beverage, real estate investors, medical professionals, entrepreneurs launching new ventures, and service-based businesses like agencies, consultants, and staffing firms. Topics range from WIP schedules and retainage cash flow for contractors to entity structuring for a physician opening a second practice location.

Every piece starts with a real question a Kinexus client asked their CPA or enrolled agent. That's why the articles read less like generic tax blog content and more like notes from an actual advisory meeting, because in most cases, they are.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## Why Kinexus clients trust this perspective

Kinexus doesn't treat tax season as a once-a-year event, and neither does Don's writing. Every article reflects the firm's year-round approach to tax planning and advisory work, the same approach clients get from Peter Williams, EA, MAcc, Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc, and Morgan Nethercott, CPA, throughout the year.

Readers get the same thinking that shows up in client meetings: proactive planning ahead of deadlines, CFO-level insight into business decisions, and a plan for handling IRS notices before they become a crisis. Don's job is translation, not invention. The strategies described in these articles are the ones Kinexus advisors already use with contractors, restaurant operators, and real estate investors in St. George and beyond.
