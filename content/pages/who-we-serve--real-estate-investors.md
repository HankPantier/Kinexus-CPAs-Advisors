## Accounting and bookkeeping for rental portfolios

Books for one rental are simple. Books for eight properties held across three LLCs are not, especially when a lender wants a trailing twelve-month P&L by Friday. Kinexus builds property-level and portfolio-level accounting so you can see performance clearly and hand off clean numbers whenever a bank, partner, or the IRS asks for them.

- Per-property income and expense tracking, so you know which units perform and which ones quietly drag down returns
- Cash flow and profit-and-loss reporting formatted for lenders, refinance applications, and partner distributions
- Multi-entity bookkeeping for investors holding property across separate LLCs, partnerships, or a holding company
- A secure digital portal for uploading leases, closing statements, and receipts from a phone or laptop, no paper folders required
- Payroll and 1099 support for investors who employ property managers, maintenance staff, or contractors directly

## Tax strategy for real estate investors

Rental income tax prep is table stakes. The real value in an investment property is what happens before the return is filed: depreciation decisions, entity elections, and exchange timing made months in advance. Shawn Gubler's CTC credential is built specifically for this kind of proactive, year-round planning, not once-a-year compliance.

### Depreciation Strategy
icon: Calculator

Proper depreciation scheduling across land, structure, and improvements affects your tax bill for the life of the property. We map it out at acquisition, not after the fact.

### 1031 Exchange Planning
icon: Building2

Deferring gains on a sale requires timing, documentation, and a qualified intermediary lined up before closing. We plan the exchange alongside your CPA, not after the contract is signed.

### Passive Activity Loss Rules
icon: Scale

Whether you qualify as a real estate professional changes what losses you can deduct against other income. We review your hours and activity logs before the IRS ever asks.

### Cost Segregation Studies
icon: ChartBar

Breaking a property into shorter-life components can accelerate depreciation and free up cash now. We coordinate the study and build it into your broader tax plan.

## Entity structuring for growing portfolios

The structure that works for one rental usually breaks down at three or four. Investors scaling toward a portfolio, or toward a fund with outside capital, need to think through liability separation, financing implications, and how gains flow to each owner before problems show up on a tax return.

Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc and Peter Williams, EA, MAcc work through single-member LLCs, series structures, holding companies, and S-corp elections for active flip businesses, weighing each option against your financing plans and exit timeline. Entity structuring is done alongside tax planning, not as a separate afterthought, so the paperwork you file with the state actually supports the return you file with the IRS.

## Support when the IRS comes calling

Depreciation schedules, passive loss claims, and cost segregation studies are exactly the kind of items that draw IRS attention. When a notice or audit letter shows up, the worst move is handling it alone or handing it to whoever prepared the return with no authority to negotiate on your behalf.

Andrew (Andy) Stewart, EA and Peter Williams, EA, MAcc hold Enrolled Agent credentials, which means they're licensed to represent you directly before the IRS on any issue, in any state. Audit protection through Kinexus covers correspondence, documentation requests, and full representation if a rental property deduction or passive activity claim gets questioned, so the notice lands on our desk instead of taking over your week.
