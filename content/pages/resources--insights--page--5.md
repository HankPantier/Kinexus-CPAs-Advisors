## Stay ahead with year-round tax planning updates

A lot of these posts exist because once-a-year tax prep leaves too much on the table. Deadlines for quarterly estimates, entity elections, and depreciation decisions happen throughout the year, and by the time an April filing rolls around, most of the useful decisions have already passed.

That's the thinking behind this blog and behind how Kinexus works with clients generally. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, RJ Mathews, CPA, and the rest of the team treat tax planning as a running conversation, checking in on numbers as they change rather than reconstructing a year after the fact. Reading through past posts is a reasonable way to get a feel for how that ongoing relationship actually works before you ever pick up the phone.
