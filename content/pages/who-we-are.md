## Your dedicated partner for business growth and wealth

Most CPA firms show up once a year, hand you a return, and disappear until next April. Kinexus works differently. We built this firm around a simple idea: the businesses and individuals who trust us with their finances deserve a partner who's paying attention in June as much as March.

That's what "Dedicated Partner for Business Growth & Wealth" actually means here. It's not a slogan we picked because it sounded good. It's the standard our CPAs, EAs, and advisors hold themselves to with every contractor, restaurant owner, physician, and real estate investor who walks through our door in St. George, Utah. Tax prep is part of the job. Building a plan around your growth, your industry, and your goals is the rest of it.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: cpa-advisor-financial-planning-session.jpg | alt: "CPA and small business owner reviewing financial documents at a desk" | query: "accountant meeting with business owner" -->
## How we approach accounting and advisory differently

A lot of firms treat accounting as compliance: file the return, close the books, move on. We treat it as an ongoing conversation. Our clients get quarterly check-ins, proactive tax strategy built around their specific entity structure, and advisory support that looks forward instead of backward.

We also run a modern, secure digital process for document handling and filing, so you're not mailing paperwork or waiting on a callback to get a straight answer. Documents move through encrypted portals, meetings happen on your schedule, and your financial data stays organized in real time rather than reconstructed at tax season.

For businesses that have outgrown a part-time bookkeeper but aren't ready to hire a full CFO, our Business Management Services fill that gap. You get CFO-level insight into cash flow, margins, and growth decisions, plus audit protection support if the IRS ever comes knocking. It's the difference between a firm that reacts to your numbers and one that helps you plan around them.

## Industry expertise that goes beyond general accounting

Generic bookkeeping misses the details that actually move the needle in your industry. A restaurant's labor cost ratios don't behave like a medical practice's revenue cycle, and a contractor's job costing has nothing in common with a real estate investor's depreciation schedule. We built our practice around five industries where that specificity matters most.

### Construction
icon: Hammer

WIP schedules, percentage-of-completion accounting, retainage, and bonding requirements shape every decision a contractor makes. We help construction clients keep job costs accurate and cash flow predictable.

### Restaurants
icon: Receipt

Thin margins, tip reporting, and multi-location tax complexity require a CPA who understands the food and beverage business, not just its tax return.

### Real Estate
icon: Home

From rental property owners to active investors, we structure entities and plan around depreciation, 1031 exchanges, and passive activity rules.

### Medical Professionals
icon: Stethoscope

Physicians, dentists, and healthcare providers need tax planning that accounts for practice ownership, equipment purchases, and high-income tax strategy.

### Service-Based Businesses
icon: Briefcase

Consultants, agencies, coaches, staffing firms, and IT providers get entity structuring and cash flow planning built for scaling revenue without scaling overhead.

## Rooted in St. George, focused on your growth

We're based in St. George, Utah, and that matters more than it might seem. Local business owners here deal with a specific mix: seasonal tourism swings, a fast-growing construction market, and a wave of entrepreneurs and real estate investors relocating from higher-cost states. We see those patterns every week, not in a textbook.

Being local also means you can sit across the table from the person handling your books, not a call center reading from a script. That accessibility, paired with a modern digital process for clients who prefer to handle everything remotely, gives St. George business owners a firm that fits how they actually want to work.
