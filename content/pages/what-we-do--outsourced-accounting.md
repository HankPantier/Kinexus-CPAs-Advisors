## A dedicated back-office team for your growing business

Most businesses hit the same wall: the bookkeeping has outgrown a part-time solution, but hiring a full-time controller doesn't pencil out yet. That gap is exactly where outsourced accounting fits. Kinexus CPAs & Advisors steps in as your back-office team, handling the day-to-day financial work so you can spend your time running the business instead of chasing invoices and reconciling accounts at 10 p.m.

This isn't tax prep with a bookkeeping add-on. It's ongoing financial management built around your business, staffed by CPAs, EAs, and accounting professionals who track your numbers month over month, not just once a year. St. George business owners in construction, restaurants, real estate, and healthcare use this service to get financial clarity without adding another full-time salary to the payroll.

Think of it as a growth partnership. As your revenue and transaction volume increase, the scope adjusts with you, no renegotiating a new vendor relationship every time you add a location or a crew.

## What's included in your outsourced accounting package

Every Kinexus outsourced accounting engagement is scoped to your transaction volume and reporting needs, but most packages include the same core building blocks:

- Transaction coding and monthly bookkeeping, categorized to your chart of accounts
- Bank and credit card reconciliations, completed monthly so your books match reality
- Accounts payable management, including bill entry, approval routing, and payment scheduling
- Accounts receivable and invoicing, so cash comes in on time instead of chasing it later
- Monthly or quarterly financial statements: profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow reports
- Payroll coordination that ties directly into your books, in step with our [payroll processing](/what-we-do/payroll) team
- A set reporting cadence, so you know exactly when to expect numbers and what they'll tell you

Compare that to a vague promise of "bookkeeping services" and the difference is clear: you know precisely what you're paying for, who's doing it, and when you'll see results. Need bookkeeping alone without the full back-office scope? Our [bookkeeping services](/what-we-do/bookkeeping) can be scaled up or down as your needs change.

## A modern, secure process that saves you time

Paper receipts in a shoebox and spreadsheets emailed back and forth are how bookkeeping used to work. Kinexus runs on cloud-based accounting software and a secure client portal, so documents move digitally from the moment you receive them to the moment they're reconciled.

You upload statements and receipts through an encrypted portal instead of a shared drive with no access controls. Reports arrive on a set schedule instead of "whenever someone gets to it." And because everything lives in the cloud, you can check your numbers from a job site, a restaurant floor, or an exam room without waiting for an email reply.

The payoff isn't just convenience. A secure, digital process protects sensitive financial data and creates a clean audit trail, which matters the moment a lender, bonding company, or the IRS asks for documentation.

## From bookkeeping to CFO-level insight as you grow

Outsourced accounting shouldn't be a service you eventually outgrow and replace. At Kinexus, it's the starting point of a relationship that expands as your business does. What begins as monthly reconciliations and financial statements can grow into full [business management services](/what-we-do/business-management-services): budgeting, forecasting, margin analysis, and the kind of strategic insight normally reserved for a CFO on payroll.

RJ Mathews, CPA, and Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc, work alongside him on client accounts that span construction, restaurants, and real estate. The result is a single firm that handles your books today and your growth strategy next year, rather than handing you off once tax season ends.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Outsourced accounting

**Q: What does outsourced accounting cost in St. George, UT?**
A: Pricing depends on transaction volume, payroll size, and reporting frequency. Kinexus scopes each engagement individually after reviewing your current books and industry, so contractors and restaurants with different complexity levels aren't charged the same flat rate.

**Q: How is outsourced accounting different from just hiring a bookkeeper?**
A: A bookkeeper typically handles data entry and reconciliations. Outsourced accounting through Kinexus adds financial statements, payroll coordination, AP/AR management, and a reporting cadence, plus access to CPAs like Shawn T. Gubler for tax and advisory questions.

**Q: Can outsourced accounting replace a full-time controller?**
A: For most businesses under a certain revenue threshold, yes. Outsourced accounting delivers the same monthly reporting and financial oversight a controller would provide, without the salary, benefits, and turnover risk of an in-house hire.

**Q: Is my financial data secure with a digital accounting process?**
A: Yes. Documents move through an encrypted client portal rather than email attachments or shared drives, and cloud-based accounting software keeps a clean, timestamped record for lenders, bonding companies, or an IRS inquiry.

**Q: Does Kinexus work with businesses outside construction and restaurants?**
A: Yes. Kinexus also serves real estate investors, medical professionals, entrepreneurs launching a business, and service-based businesses like consultants and agencies, tailoring the accounting workflow to each industry's specific reporting needs.
