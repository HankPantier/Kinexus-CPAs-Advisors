## Turn your back office into a growth engine

Most growing businesses hit the same wall. Revenue climbs, headcount grows, and the back office keeps running on the same spreadsheets, sticky notes, and one overworked office manager from three years ago. Bookkeeping, payroll, and reporting processes that worked at $500,000 in revenue start breaking at $3 million.

Kinexus CPAs & Advisors built this service around a simple idea: your accounting infrastructure should scale with your business, not lag behind it. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, has spent 16 years managing accounting and operations functions for growing companies before turning that experience into a consulting practice for St. George business owners. This isn't theoretical process improvement. It's a CPA firm applying its own operating playbook to your back office.

## Signs your back office is holding your business back

Back office drag rarely announces itself. It shows up as a gut feeling that something's off, or a pile of small frustrations that add up to real cost.

- You're closing the books two or three weeks late every month, so decisions get made on stale numbers
- Bookkeeping, payroll, and invoicing live in three different systems that don't talk to each other
- The owner still approves every invoice, reconciles every account, or runs payroll personally
- Nobody can tell you gross margin by job, project, or location without a week of digging
- You've hired administrative help, but tasks still fall through the cracks
- Financial reporting tells you what happened last quarter, not what's happening now

If two or more of these sound familiar, the problem usually isn't effort. It's structure.

## How we get your operations running smoothly

### Assessment
We spend time in your current systems and talk to the people who run them, from your bookkeeper to your office manager, to understand exactly where the friction is.

### Roadmap
You get a prioritized plan: what to fix first, what software or staffing changes make sense, and a realistic timeline. No 40-page report that sits in a drawer.

### Implementation
We help set up new systems, document new processes, and train the people who'll use them, working alongside your existing accounting or payroll provider when needed.

### Ongoing check-ins
Back offices drift again if nobody's watching. We schedule regular reviews to make sure the new process is actually sticking and adjust as your business keeps growing.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Back office operations efficiency consulting
