## Bookkeeping built for growing St. George businesses

Ask five local firms what bookkeeping means and you'll get five different answers. For too many, it's a once-a-month data entry task handled by whoever's available, then filed away until tax season. At Kinexus CPAs & Advisors, bookkeeping is the starting point for proactive tax planning, cash flow decisions, and the CFO-level insight that helps St. George business owners grow. Every transaction recorded and every account reconciled feeds directly into the advisory work our CPAs and enrolled agents do year-round, not just in March and April. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and the Kinexus team built this process around a modern, secure digital workflow, so your financials stay accurate, current, and ready to support real decisions. If your books are a mess, or you're outgrowing a solo bookkeeper working out of a spreadsheet, this is where the fix starts.

## What our bookkeeping service includes

Bookkeeping only works if it's consistent, accurate, and built on a system you can actually see into. Our process covers the full back-office cycle:

- Transaction classification and coding, so every dollar lands in the right account
- Expense tracking and categorization for cleaner reporting and easier tax prep
- Bill pay and accounts payable management, so nothing slips past due
- Invoicing and accounts receivable follow-up to keep cash coming in
- Monthly account reconciliation across bank, credit card, and loan accounts

Every client works inside a secure digital portal for document handling and file sharing, not folders of paper receipts or email attachments. That's a different experience than the paper-based, spreadsheet-driven setup many small local bookkeepers still run on. You get real-time visibility into your numbers, a documented audit trail, and a bookkeeping file your CPA can build tax strategy from, because RJ Mathews, CPA, and Peter Williams, EA, MAcc, work from the same clean data you do.

## Bookkeeping tailored to your industry

Generic bookkeeping misses the details that matter most to your business. Ours doesn't.

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Job costing, retainage tracking, and work-in-progress schedules require a bookkeeper who understands percentage-of-completion accounting, not just basic expense coding. Project-level costs get tracked as the job progresses, not reconstructed after it's done.

### Restaurants
icon: Receipt

Daily sales reconciliation, tip reporting, food and labor cost percentages, and multi-location tracking need a bookkeeping rhythm built for restaurant margins, not a once-a-month catch-up.

### Real Estate Investors
icon: Home

Rental income, security deposits, property-level profit and loss statements, and depreciation schedules get tracked separately for each property, so you know which assets are actually performing.

### Medical Professionals
icon: Stethoscope

Insurance reimbursement timing, payroll for clinical and administrative staff, and equipment financing all get accounted for with the cash flow patterns specific to a practice.

### Service-Based Businesses
icon: Briefcase

Consultants, agencies, coaches, staffing firms, and IT providers need clean recurring revenue tracking and project-based cost allocation to price services profitably.

## From bookkeeping to CFO-level insight

Clean books are the beginning, not the destination. Once your bookkeeping is accurate and current, it becomes the data set for real strategy: cash flow forecasting, tax planning that happens across the year instead of one rushed conversation in April, and the kind of business management services that tell you which of your service lines actually makes money. Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc, and Morgan Nethercott, CPA, work directly with bookkeeping clients to build that bridge, so a first-time business owner who started with basic transaction tracking can move into CFO-level guidance without switching firms or re-explaining the business from scratch. Your bookkeeper and your tax advisor are the same team, working off the same numbers.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Bookkeeping

**Q: How much does bookkeeping cost in St. George, UT?**
A: Cost depends on transaction volume, number of accounts, and industry complexity. A construction business with job costing needs a different scope than a single-location service business. Kinexus reviews your current setup during a consultation and quotes a monthly rate based on actual needs, not a flat package.

**Q: What's the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?**
A: Bookkeeping is the day-to-day recording of transactions, reconciliations, invoicing, and bill pay. Accounting uses that data for tax planning, financial statements, and strategic decisions. At Kinexus, the same firm handles both, so your bookkeeping feeds directly into CPA-level tax and advisory work.

**Q: Can Kinexus clean up bookkeeping that's fallen behind?**
A: Yes. Catch-up and cleanup bookkeeping is a common starting point for new clients, especially business owners who've been managing books themselves or through a bookkeeper who fell behind. The team reconciles historical records before setting up ongoing monthly processes.

**Q: Do you offer bookkeeping for specific industries like construction or restaurants?**
A: Yes. Kinexus handles industry-specific bookkeeping for construction (job costing, retainage), restaurants (daily sales reconciliation, tip reporting), real estate investors (property-level P&Ls), medical practices, and service-based businesses, rather than applying one generic template to every client.
