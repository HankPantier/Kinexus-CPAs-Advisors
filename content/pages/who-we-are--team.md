## A dedicated partner behind every client relationship

Most CPA firms meet you once a year, hand you a return, and disappear until next April. That's not how Kinexus works. Every credentialed advisor on this team, from the CPAs who structure your entity to the Enrolled Agents who stand between you and the IRS, treats tax and accounting as a year-round conversation, not a once-a-year transaction.

We're based in St. George, and we built this team around the businesses that actually operate here: contractors pouring foundations in Washington County, restaurant owners managing food costs on Main Street, real estate investors closing on rentals across southern Utah, and medical practices trying to keep more of what they bill. Here's who you'll be working with.

<!-- block: team-grid | variant: 2-col -->
## Leadership and advisory team

### Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc
Founding Partner

Shawn holds a CPA license, a Master of Accounting, and a Certified Tax Coach (CTC) designation, a credential fewer than a few thousand advisors nationwide carry because it requires ongoing training specifically in proactive tax reduction strategy. That's the lens he brings to every engagement: not how to file a return correctly, but how to structure a business so the tax bill is smaller before the year even ends. He works closely with construction and real estate clients, where entity structure and timing decisions carry outsized tax consequences.

### RJ Mathews, CPA
Partner

RJ focuses on the advisory side of the practice, translating financial statements into decisions business owners can actually act on. Restaurant and service-based business clients rely on him for margin analysis, cash flow forecasting, and the kind of month-over-month guidance that keeps a growing business from outrunning its own books.

### Chase Gubler, CPA, MAcc
Partner

Chase pairs his CPA license with a Master of Accounting and works primarily with entrepreneurs launching new ventures and established businesses scaling into their next phase. He's often the first call for clients weighing entity structure, growth financing, or an acquisition.

### Morgan Nethercott, CPA
Partner

Morgan leads client engagements in medical and professional practices, where compliance complexity and staffing costs demand a CPA who understands the operational side of running a practice, not just the tax side of owning one.

<!-- block: team-grid | variant: 2-col -->
## Tax strategy and resolution specialists

### Andrew Stewart, EA
Enrolled Agent

Known around the office as Andy, Andrew holds the Enrolled Agent (EA) credential, which grants him unlimited rights to represent clients directly before the IRS. When a client receives a notice, gets flagged for an audit, or needs someone to negotiate a resolution on their behalf, Andy is the specialist handling it, backed by the firm's Audit Protection service.

### Peter Williams, EA, MAcc
Enrolled Agent

Peter combines his EA credential with a Master of Accounting, giving him both the representation authority to deal with the IRS and the technical depth to build multi-year tax strategies for growing businesses. He works alongside Shawn on advanced planning engagements where entity choice, retirement contributions, and depreciation strategy intersect.

Between Andy, Peter, and Shawn's Certified Tax Coach credential, clients get a planning team built for two jobs most firms treat separately: keeping the tax bill low all year, and having someone qualified to fight for you if the IRS ever sends a letter.

## Accounting, bookkeeping, and payroll specialists

Behind every CPA and EA at Kinexus is a team that handles the daily mechanics: transaction coding, bill pay, invoicing, and payroll runs that have to land on time, every time. Leslye Haws, one of the firm's bookkeeping specialists, is the person many clients talk to most often, because she's the one keeping their books current between the quarterly planning calls.

This team runs on the same modern, secure digital process the rest of the firm uses: cloud-based accounting platforms, encrypted document portals, and payroll systems that handle employee setup and salary changes without a stack of paper. It's a quieter job than tax strategy, but it's the reason the numbers your CPA advises on are actually accurate.

## Industry knowledge woven into every engagement

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Job costing, retainage, and WIP schedules require an advisor who's seen a percentage-of-completion report before. Our construction clients work with team members who understand bonding requirements and how a single change order can shift a project's margin.

### Restaurants
icon: Receipt

Food and labor costs move weekly, not annually. The team members serving restaurant and food & beverage operators build tax and cash flow plans around that reality, including multi-location tax strategy for growing concepts.

### Real estate and real estate investors
icon: Building2

Cost segregation, 1031 exchanges, and rental portfolio structuring show up constantly in this practice. Clients buying, holding, or flipping property work directly with advisors who model these decisions before closing, not after.

### Medical professionals
icon: Stethoscope

Physicians, dentists, and healthcare providers get advisors who understand practice overhead, staffing ratios, and entity structures built for high-income professionals with real liability exposure.

### Service-based businesses
icon: Briefcase

Consultants, agencies, coaches, staffing firms, and IT providers work with team members focused on scaling recurring revenue without letting overhead or owner compensation eat the upside.

## Grown in St. George, invested in your success

This isn't a regional office of a national firm parachuting in advisors who rotate out every few years. The people on this team live in Washington County, know the local permitting offices and lenders their construction clients deal with, and stick around long enough to watch a client's business grow from a startup to a multi-location operation.

That tenure matters more than it sounds. A bookkeeper who's worked with the same restaurant client for years catches a cost overrun faster than someone seeing the books for the first time. A CPA who's structured a dozen local real estate deals spots the tax angle a generalist misses. Kinexus was built to be the Dedicated Partner for Business Growth & Wealth its tagline promises, and that starts with keeping the same faces on your account year after year.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Our team

**Q: Does the team have experience with specific industries?**
A: Yes. Team members are aligned to construction, restaurants, real estate and real estate investors, medical professionals, and service-based businesses, so clients work with an advisor familiar with their industry's specific tax and operational issues.

**Q: How long has the Kinexus team worked in St. George, UT?**
A: The team is based locally in Washington County and known for long employee tenure, meaning clients typically work with the same bookkeeper, CPA, or EA over multiple years rather than rotating staff.
