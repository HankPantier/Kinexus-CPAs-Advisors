## Everything you need for a smooth engagement

Tax season shouldn't mean digging through file cabinets or mailing sensitive paperwork across town. This page gathers the forms, checklists, and links Kinexus clients ask for most, from new client paperwork to IRS resources, all organized in one place. Whether you're starting your first engagement with our team or gathering documents for this year's return, everything here connects back to a secure, digital process built for business owners and individuals in St. George and across Washington County.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: client-portal-document-upload.jpg | alt: "Client uploading tax documents through a secure online portal on a laptop" | query: "person using laptop online portal" -->
## Client portal and secure document upload

Most of what you need to send us doesn't belong in an envelope. Our secure client portal lets you upload W-2s, 1099s, financial statements, and signed authorization forms from a phone, tablet, or laptop, day or night. Documents move through encrypted channels instead of email attachments or postal mail, and you can track what's been received in real time.

If you're a new client, your onboarding packet, including engagement letters and intake forms, arrives through the same portal. Existing clients use it year-round for tax documents, payroll records, and e-signatures. Ready to get set up? [Contact our team](/contact) and we'll send your portal invitation the same day.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-left | image: new-client-onboarding-paperwork.jpg | alt: "New client reviewing onboarding paperwork with a CPA advisor" | query: "client meeting signing documents office" -->
## New client onboarding forms

Starting service with Kinexus, whether for accounting, tax, or payroll, usually involves a short stack of paperwork. Here's what most new clients complete before we begin:

- Engagement letter outlining the scope of your accounting, tax, or payroll service
- New client intake questionnaire covering business structure, prior accountant contact, and financial history
- IRS Power of Attorney (Form 2848), prepared when our Enrolled Agents need to speak directly with the IRS on your behalf
- ACH authorization for payroll direct deposit or fee payment
- Prior-year tax returns and financial statements, uploaded through your secure portal

All of it moves through the same digital process, no printer or fax machine required.

## Tax preparation checklists and organizers

Different businesses collect different paperwork, and a generic checklist rarely covers what a contractor, physician, or restaurant owner actually needs. These checklists are built around the industries we work with most.

### Individual Tax Prep Checklist
icon: FileText

W-2s, 1099s, mortgage interest statements, and last year's return. Our [personal income tax](/what-we-do/personal-income-tax) checklist covers documents individuals commonly overlook, from HSA contributions to childcare expenses.

### Business Owner Checklist
icon: Briefcase

Profit and loss statements, mileage logs, and prior quarterly estimates. Pairs with our [business tax](/what-we-do/business-tax) planning process so nothing gets missed before year-end.

### Construction and Contractors
icon: Hammer

Job costing reports, retainage schedules, and equipment purchase records. See how these tie into [construction](/who-we-serve/construction) accounting and WIP reporting.

### Real Estate Investors
icon: Home

Rent rolls, depreciation schedules, and 1031 exchange documentation for every property. Built for [real estate investors](/who-we-serve/real-estate-investors) managing multiple entities.

### Medical Professionals
icon: Stethoscope

Practice P&L statements, equipment depreciation, and entity distribution records for [medical professionals](/who-we-serve/medical-professionals) running a practice alongside patient care.

### Restaurants and Food Service
icon: Receipt

Tip reporting, food cost percentages, and multi-location P&Ls for [restaurant](/who-we-serve/restaurants) owners juggling thin margins and seasonal staffing.

## Payroll and bookkeeping resource links

Setting up payroll or cleaning up your books usually means chasing down the same handful of federal forms. We keep the ones our [payroll](/what-we-do/payroll) and [bookkeeping](/what-we-do/bookkeeping) clients request most in one place:

- Form W-4 for new employee withholding elections
- Form W-9 for independent contractors and 1099 vendors
- Form I-9 for employment eligibility verification
- Direct deposit authorization for payroll setup
- Chart of accounts template for bookkeeping and QuickBooks setup

Ask your Kinexus contact for the current version of any of these; requirements and formats update periodically.

## IRS and government resource links

When a notice arrives from the IRS or the Utah State Tax Commission, having the right link matters. Here's where our team sends clients most often:

- IRS.gov for federal forms, instructions, and current filing deadlines
- IRS Direct Pay and EFTPS for federal tax payments
- Utah State Tax Commission (tap.utah.gov) for state filings and payment history
- IRS Where's My Refund tool for return status

If a letter shows up that you don't understand, don't respond to it alone. Our Enrolled Agents, including Peter Williams, EA, MAcc, and Andrew Stewart, EA, handle [IRS representation and tax resolution](/services/irs-representation-tax-resolution) and [audit protection](/what-we-do/audit-protection) for clients across St. George.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Forms documents and links

**Q: How do I securely send documents to Kinexus CPAs?**
A: Use the Kinexus client portal to upload tax documents, financial statements, and signed forms. It's encrypted, available anytime, and replaces mailing or emailing sensitive paperwork. Contact our team if you need a portal invitation set up.

**Q: What paperwork do I need to become a new client?**
A: New clients typically complete an engagement letter, intake questionnaire, and, if IRS representation is needed, a Power of Attorney form. Existing tax returns and financial statements are uploaded through the secure portal during onboarding.

**Q: Do you have tax checklists for specific industries?**
A: Yes. Kinexus provides checklists tailored to construction, real estate investors, medical professionals, restaurants, and general business owners, reflecting the specific documents each industry needs for accurate, complete tax preparation.

**Q: Where can I find IRS or Utah state tax forms?**
A: Our IRS and government resource links section points to IRS.gov, EFTPS, Utah's Taxpayer Access Point, and refund status tools. For anything more complex, our Enrolled Agents can handle representation directly.

**Q: What if I can't find the form I'm looking for?**
A: Schedule a consultation and our team will identify the exact form, checklist, or portal link you need for your situation, rather than leaving you to search on your own.
