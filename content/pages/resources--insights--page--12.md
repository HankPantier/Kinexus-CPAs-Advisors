### Retainage cash flow strategies for contractors

### Reading a WIP schedule the way your banker does
photo: wip-schedule-review.jpg
query: construction manager reviewing financial report

A percentage-of-completion schedule tells a lender more about your business than your bank balance does. Here's what they check line by line before renewing a bond or line of credit.

### Fraud prevention for nonprofit boards

### Selling a business, rental, or large asset this year

### Is your business structure costing you money
photo: entity-structure-review.jpg

An LLC taxed the wrong way, or an S corp election made too late, can cost thousands a year in avoidable tax. Here's how to check your setup.

### Zero-based budgeting during persistent inflation

## Get proactive tax and business advice year-round

Most of what's on this page didn't come out of a once-a-year filing season conversation. It came from client meetings held in June, October, and February, because tax and business decisions don't wait for April. Shawn T. Gubler, CPA, CTC, MAcc, and the rest of the Kinexus team build planning into the calendar, not just the deadline.

That's the difference between a firm that files a return and one that helps decide, before December 31, whether to buy equipment, change an entity structure, or add a fifth employee. Documents move through a secure digital portal instead of a filing cabinet, and CFO-level reporting through [Business Management Services](/what-we-do/business-management-services) turns monthly numbers into decisions rather than a static compliance record.
