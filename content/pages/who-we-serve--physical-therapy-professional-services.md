## Financial challenges unique to physical therapy and professional practices

Practices like yours run into money problems that a restaurant or retail shop never sees. We hear the same issues from clinic owners across Washington County, and they usually fall into a few categories.

- Insurance reimbursement delays that create a gap between the visit and the deposit, sometimes 30 to 60 days out
- Payroll that mixes W-2 clinicians, 1099 contract therapists, and front-office staff, each with different tax treatment
- Equipment financing decisions for treatment tables, modalities, and build-outs that affect both cash flow and depreciation strategy
- Multi-location growth that multiplies bookkeeping, payroll runs, and state licensing compliance overnight
- Ongoing compliance costs tied to state licensing boards, HIPAA, and professional liability requirements

None of these are solved by tax software or a once-a-year filing appointment. They require someone watching the numbers between visits, not just at year-end.
