## LinkedIn

A builder who signs a stack of new home contracts in January 2026 without checking their tax method election could default right back into percentage-of-completion, even if the contract now qualifies for something better. OBBBA expands the home-construction exception under IRC Section 460, so more residential projects, including some multi-unit developments, can now use completed contract accounting instead of PCM. For spec builders carrying unsold inventory or waiting 12 to 18 months on buyer financing to close, that's the difference between a tax bill before the cash arrives and one that actually lines up with it. The catch: this applies to contracts signed after enactment, and the window to review eligibility is before 2026 contracts get signed, not during tax season. Shawn Gubler, CPA, CTC, and the Kinexus construction team already build WIP schedules and job-costing detail for contractors across Washington County. Read the full breakdown, including the 80% cost threshold and the Form 3115 filing question, here: https://stgcpas.com/resources/obbbas-expanded-home-construction-exception-why-builders-sho
