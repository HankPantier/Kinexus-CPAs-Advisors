## LinkedIn

A St. George excavation contractor paid in full for a skid steer on December 28. It didn't arrive and start moving dirt until January 6. That 8-day gap pushed a $65,000 deduction into the wrong tax year, and it cost him real cash the following April.

Here's the detail most contractors miss: the IRS goes by the placed-in-service date, not the invoice date or the day you paid. Bonus depreciation is back to 100% for property acquired after January 19, 2025, but that only helps if the equipment is delivered and operational by December 31.

If you're a contractor in Washington County planning a Q4 equipment purchase, this is worth a projection call before you sign the purchase order, not after. We work through Section 179 versus bonus depreciation timing with construction clients every December.
